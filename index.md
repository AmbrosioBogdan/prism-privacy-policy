# PRISM – Privacy Policy

**Last updated: November 3, 2026**

## 1. Introduction

This Privacy Policy explains how personal information is handled when you use PRISM, a mobile arcade game developed by Bogdan Ambrosio (the "Developer").

PRISM is a single-player game distributed through Google Play.

For privacy-related questions or requests, you can contact the Developer at:

**Email:** bogdan.soldi07@gmail.com

## 2. Information We Collect

PRISM does not require an account or registration.

The game does not ask players to provide their name, nickname, email address, or profile information to play.

The Developer does not operate:
- Online player accounts.
- Online leaderboards.
- Chat or messaging features.
- Social networking features.
- Cloud-based game saves.

Game progress and settings are stored locally on the player's device.

The game does not request access to precise GPS location for gameplay.

## 3. Advertising – Google Mobile Ads

PRISM uses Google's Google Mobile Ads SDK to request and display advertisements.

When advertising services are enabled, Google and its advertising partners may automatically collect or process certain information, including:

- IP address, which may be used to estimate general location.
- Advertising identifiers, such as the Android Advertising ID, where permitted.
- Device and app identifiers.
- App interactions and advertising interactions.
- Information about advertisements displayed or viewed.
- Diagnostic and performance information, including app stability and performance.

This information may be used for:
- Delivering advertisements.
- Measuring advertising performance.
- Limiting advertising frequency.
- Preventing fraud and abuse.
- Advertising personalisation, where legally permitted and where the required consent or other legal basis exists.

Advertising practices may differ depending on the user's age, location, privacy choices, and applicable laws.

Google may share information with advertising technology providers involved in delivering and measuring advertisements.

For more information, please consult:

[Google Privacy Policy](https://policies.google.com/privacy)

[Google Mobile Ads SDK documentation](https://developers.google.com/admob/unity/privacy)

## 4. Consent Management – Google UMP

PRISM integrates Google's User Messaging Platform (UMP) SDK for managing privacy and consent messages.

Where required, users may be presented with options to:
- Consent to the processing of personal information.
- Refuse consent.
- Manage individual privacy preferences.

The consent management system may store privacy choices on the device and communicate relevant consent information to Google and participating advertising partners.

Users may be able to review or change their privacy choices through the privacy options made available in the game.

More information:

[Google UMP documentation](https://developers.google.com/admob/unity/privacy)

## 5. Children and Family Audiences

PRISM is intended for a general audience and may be used by children and adults.

The game does not provide chat, messaging, public profiles, or social interaction features.

The Developer intends to configure advertising services in accordance with applicable child-privacy laws and Google Play Families requirements.

Where required, advertising requests for children and users of unknown age must receive the appropriate child-directed or age-restricted treatment.

Advertising personalisation and the use of advertising identifiers must be restricted where required by applicable laws and Google Play policies.

Only permitted advertising SDK versions and advertising formats will be used when serving advertisements to children.

Parents or legal guardians may contact the Developer with questions or concerns about the handling of information through PRISM:

**Email:** bogdan.soldi07@gmail.com

## 6. In-App Purchases

In-app purchases are not currently enabled in PRISM.

If purchase functionality is introduced, transactions will be processed through Google Play Billing.

Payment information, including payment card details, is handled by Google Play. The Developer does not receive or store full payment card details.

This section will be updated if purchase functionality is introduced.

[Google Privacy Policy](https://policies.google.com/privacy)

## 7. Legal Bases for Processing

Depending on the type of processing and applicable law, personal information may be processed on the following legal bases:

- Consent, where required for advertising or other processing activities.
- Performance of a service requested by the user.
- Compliance with legal obligations.
- Legitimate interests, where permitted by applicable law and where those interests do not override the user's rights and freedoms.

Users may withdraw consent where processing is based on consent.

## 8. Data Retention

Game progress and settings are stored locally on the user's device.

This information generally remains on the device until the game data is cleared or the application is uninstalled.

Consent information may be stored by the UMP SDK to remember privacy choices.

Information collected independently by Google is retained according to Google's own data retention policies.

The Developer does not control Google's independent retention periods.

## 9. Third-Party Services

These services may process information according to their own privacy policies.

The Developer does not sell players' personal information.
